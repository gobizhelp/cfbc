The Truth Nobody Says Out Loud

What Most People Are Actually Afraid Of.

Nine out of ten people who want to try CrossFit don't — because of these three fears. Here's exactly how CFBC solves each one before you even step on the floor.

Fear #1

"I'm Not In Shape Enough."

"I'll be the slowest one there. Everyone will stare. I'll hold back the class."

The Fix

Your first 5 classes are built for your body today, not the athlete you imagine other members to be. 68% of our current members started exactly where you are: zero gym experience.

Fear #2

"I'm Going To Get Hurt."

"CrossFit is dangerous. People throw up. I'll wreck my back on day one."

The Fix

Every rep of every movement gets a form check from a Level-2 certified coach. We scale before we load. Zero reported injuries in our on-ramp program. Ever.

Fear #3

"I Won't Know What To Do."

"There'll be a board with codes on it. I won't know the movements. I'll be lost."

The Fix

Every class starts with a 10-minute whiteboard walkthrough. We demonstrate every movement, explain the "why," and your coach stays next to you if you need extra attention.

Still Nervous?

The Questions Every Beginner Asks Us.

Do I need to be in shape to start?

No. The opposite, actually. 68% of our members started with zero gym experience. Our on-ramp is designed for day-one bodies. We meet you where you are.

What if I'm the oldest / slowest / heaviest?

Our members range from 18 to 71. Every WOD scales to 3 levels. Nobody finishes "last" — we finish together. Most beginners report being surprised by how quickly this stops being a concern.

Is CrossFit safe?

Done badly, any workout is unsafe. Done with coaching, it's one of the safest ways to train. We have a zero-injury record on our on-ramp because we scale before we load. Always.

What should I wear / bring?

Athletic clothes you can move in. Flat shoes (sneakers are fine — no running shoes for lifting days, but we'll guide you). A water bottle. That's it. We provide everything else.

Can I cancel if it's not a fit?

Anytime. No contract, no cancellation fee, no ghost-billing. If CFBC isn't right in 30 days, we part as friends. Most don't — but the door's always open.

I've tried gyms before and always quit. Why will this work?

Because it's not about willpower. It's about environment. Our retention rate is 87% after year 1 because the coaching + community architecture does the heavy lifting. You just have to show up the first day.
